Biography

I'm Olachi Agwu, a physiologist just beginning my journey toward becoming a tech guru. I live by the words often attributed to Leonardo da Vinci: Learning never exhausts the mind. That belief is exactly why I joined this program: to build a real foundation in full-stack development.

Why AI Powered FullStack Engineering

I've always been the person who wonders what's happening behind the screen when an app loads, a payment goes through, or a feed refreshes. As a physiologist, I'm used to studying how complex systems work, and I think that's why I'm so drawn to the creativity and problem-solving behind the products developers build. I'm inspired by people like Ada Lovelace, often regarded as the first computer programmer, and by the women in tech who keep pushing what's possible today.
I joined this program because I'm done only admiring that work from the outside. I want to build it myself: the frontend people actually interact with, the backend and databases behind it, the APIs that connect them, and the AI that makes an app smarter. My goal is to turn my curiosity into a skill set I can use to build things that genuinely help people, and to grow into a confident, well-rounded full-stack engineer.

My Goals for the Program

  • Build things, not just study them: I want to ship working, interactive apps instead of only understanding concepts on paper.
  • Master both sides of the stack: I want to be confident on the frontend and backend and see how databases and APIs fit into one real product.
  • Learn to work with AI, not around it: I want to use AI tools well in development and learn to build AI into apps people actually use.
  • Finish with projects I can point to: I want real work to show for my time here, and I'm okay with breaking things along the way, since debugging my own mistakes is how I'll sharpen my problem-solving.
  • Leave with the confidence to start from nothing: The goal is to take an idea and turn it into a complete application, and to keep growing as a full-stack engineer long after the program ends.
